## Chemical Dosage & Pest Management Safety Standards

1. **Pre-harvest Interval (PHI)**: Farmers must strictly observe the mandatory waiting period between the last spray and crop harvest as printed on the CIBRC product label.
2. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Protective gloves, face masks, and eye goggles must be worn during preparation and foliar spraying of chemical formulations.
3. **Local KVK Consultation**: Extension workers must cross-reference regional pesticide registrations with the State Agriculture University (SAU) or local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before approving large-scale spraying.
4. **Kisan Call Centre Toll-Free Helpline**: Farmers requiring real-time clarification can call `1800-180-1551` (toll-free, 6 AM to 10 PM daily in 22 languages).
